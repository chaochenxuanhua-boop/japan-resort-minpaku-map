180-Day Minpaku (住宅宿泊事業法)

National registration caps unhosted stays at 180 nights/year, and Prefectural or Municipal Article 18 ordinances often reduce usable days to 60–115 nights via summer blackouts or weekday bans.

365-Day Simple Lodging (旅館業法・簡易宿所)

A Public Health Center (Hokenjo) commercial permit unlocks 365-day operation when parcel zoning, commercial fire alarms (Jikahō), and check-in rules (<200 sqm building threshold) are met.
