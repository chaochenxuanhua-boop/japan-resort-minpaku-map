Frequently Asked Questions: Ishigaki Short-Term Rentals

Can foreigners run Airbnb 365 days in Ishigaki?

Yes, foreign investors can legally purchase freehold property in Ishigaki (Okinawa) with zero nationality restrictions, but operating 365 days per year requires obtaining a Hotel and Ryokan Business Act Simple Lodging (Kan'i Shukusho / 簡易宿所) commercial permit from Okinawa Prefecture Hygiene Division & Yaeyama Public Health Center (沖縄県 / 八重山保健所). Current 365-day feasibility in Ishigaki is rated 'High - Investor Friendly': Northern coastal villa belts (Kabira, Yonehara, Nosoko, Fusaki) lie largely outside restrictive residential zoning, enabling 365-day Simple Lodging pool villas—provided Agricultural Land Act (Nochi-ho Article 4/5 conversion) and Natural Park buffers are cleared pre-purchase.

What are Ishigaki Minpaku ordinance caps and restricted seasons?

Under Japan's Private Lodging Business Act (Article 18 local ordinance authority), Ishigaki is classified as 'Amber: Zone/Season Restricted', resulting in ~165 effective operating days per year (versus the 180-day national ceiling). Specifically: Okinawa Prefectural Minpaku Ordinance restricts weekday operation within 100m of schools on school days. Extended March-November subtropical beach weather makes a 180-day ceiling severely yield-limiting compared to a 365-day Simple Lodging villa license.
