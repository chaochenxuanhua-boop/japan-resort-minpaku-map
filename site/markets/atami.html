What are Atami Minpaku ordinance caps and restricted seasons?

Under Japan's Private Lodging Business Act (Article 18 local ordinance authority), Atami is classified as 'Amber: Zone/Season Restricted', resulting in ~140 effective operating days per year (versus the 180-day national ceiling). Specifically: Shizuoka Prefectural Minpaku Ordinance restricts weekday operation within school perimeters and designated residential/villa covenant zones. Crucially, many Atami ocean-view villas sit inside managed bessochi estates whose private HOA bylaws restrict or levy commercial lodging fees.
