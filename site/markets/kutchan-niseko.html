Statutory Zoning, Fire Code & Official Ordinance Breakdown

Competent authority: Hokkaido Government & Kutchan Public Health Center (北海道経済部観光局 / 倶知安保健所) · Verified 2026-11-26

1. Private Lodging Act (180d Minpaku) & Article 18 Rules

Governed by the Hokkaido Prefectural Minpaku Ordinance: unhosted Minpaku is prohibited on school days within 100m of elementary and junior high school entrances in Kutchan Town. Outside school buffers in Hirafu and Kabayama resort zones, up to 180 days apply subject to winter snow-clearing and registered local administrator (Kanri Gyosha) response rules.

2. 365-Day Ryokan-gyo (簡易宿所) Parcel Feasibility

Hirafu Quasi-City Planning Districts and Niseko resort parcels allow 365-day Simple Lodging (Kan'i Shukusho) subject to Kutchan Landscape & Machizukuri Ordinance height/FAR limits and neighbor briefings. Institutional chalets routinely secure full Ryokan-gyo permits to capture ultra-prime winter ADR plus green-season golf stays.

3. Fire Service Act (Jikahō) & Building Standards

Kutchan Public Health Center & Shiribeshi Fire Department enforce winter snow-protected egress, wired Jikaho automatic fire alarms, >=3.3 sqm per guest floor area, and face-to-face or compliant real-time ICT video check-in backed by 10-minute on-site emergency dispatch.

4. Accommodation Tax & Official Citations

Kutchan Town Accommodation Tax: 3% flat rate of room charge plus Hokkaido prefectural lodging levy.
