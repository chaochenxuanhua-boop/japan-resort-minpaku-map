1. Private Lodging Act (180d Minpaku) & Article 18 Rules

Yamanashi Prefecture imposes no Article 18 statutory day-reduction ordinance; full 180-day operation is permitted. However, intense year-round inbound Mt. Fuji demand exhausts 180 nights in 6 months, leaving half the year idle unless upgraded to a 365-day Simple Lodging license.

2. 365-Day Ryokan-gyo (簡易宿所) Parcel Feasibility

High feasibility across non-delineated (Hisenbiki) Urban Planning parcels around Lake Kawaguchi and Saiko, subject to Fujikawaguchiko Landscape Ordinance, Mt. Fuji World Heritage buffer height/color rules, and municipal prior neighbor consultation.

3. Fire Service Act (Jikahō) & Building Standards

Fuji-Tobu Public Health Center & Kawaguchiko Fire HQ inspect Jikaho alarms, propane gas interlocks, septic Johkasou upsizing (where sewer lines are absent), and freeze-protected piping.

4. Accommodation Tax & Official Citations
