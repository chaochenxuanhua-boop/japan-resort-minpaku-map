Can foreigners run Airbnb 365 days in Naha?

Yes, foreign investors can legally purchase freehold property in Naha (Okinawa) with zero nationality restrictions, but operating 365 days per year requires obtaining a Hotel and Ryokan Business Act Simple Lodging (Kan'i Shukusho / 簡易宿所) commercial permit from Naha City Public Health Center Environmental Hygiene Division (那覇市保健所環境衛生課). Current 365-day feasibility in Naha is rated 'High - Investor Friendly': Commercial and Neighborhood Commercial districts along Kokusai-dori, Makishi, Miebashi, and Tomari ferry port (gateway to Kerama Islands) readily qualify for 365-day Simple Lodging condo-hotels and whole-floor family suites.

What are Naha Minpaku ordinance caps and restricted seasons?

Under Japan's Private Lodging Business Act (Article 18 local ordinance authority), Naha is classified as 'Amber: Zone/Season Restricted', resulting in ~160 effective operating days per year (versus the 180-day national ceiling). Specifically: Naha City operates an independent municipal Public Health Center: Minpaku is restricted on school days within 100m of schools and inside designated residential pockets, with mandatory 10-minute on-island administrator response and strict typhoon/garbage compliance.
