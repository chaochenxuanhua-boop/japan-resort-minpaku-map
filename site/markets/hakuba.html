Statutory Zoning, Fire Code & Official Ordinance Breakdown

Competent authority: Nagano Prefecture Tourism & Omachi Public Health Center (長野県観光スポーツ部 / 大町保健所) · Verified 2026-11-26

1. Private Lodging Act (180d Minpaku) & Article 18 Rules

Nagano Prefectural Article 18 Ordinance prohibits unhosted Minpaku statewide during all of May (May 1-31) and July 1-September 30 (green-season blackout), plus bans Monday noon through Saturday noon operation inside designated residential zones and within 100m of schools on school days.

2. 365-Day Ryokan-gyo (簡易宿所) Parcel Feasibility

Highly viable across Wadano, Echoland, Happo, and Goryu. Vast portions of Hakuba Village are Quasi-City Planning or unzoned alpine parcels without exclusive residential hotel bans, making 365-day Simple Lodging the standard vehicle to unlock summer MTB/hiking revenue and unlimited winter powder nights.

3. Fire Service Act (Jikahō) & Building Standards

Requires commercial automatic fire alarm system (Jikaho), emergency exit illumination, fire-retardant curtains/carpets, snow-load egress clearance, and Omachi Public Health Center inspection. Buildings exceeding 200 sqm converting from residential to lodging require Building Code change-of-use confirmation.

4. Accommodation Tax & Official Citations

Nagano Prefecture + Hakuba Village accommodation tax framework applies (flat ¥200-¥300/person/night with low-rate exemptions).
