What are Furano Minpaku ordinance caps and restricted seasons?

Under Japan's Private Lodging Business Act (Article 18 local ordinance authority), Furano is classified as 'Amber: Zone/Season Restricted', resulting in ~165 effective operating days per year (versus the 180-day national ceiling).
