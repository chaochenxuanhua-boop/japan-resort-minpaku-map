1. Private Lodging Act (180d Minpaku) & Article 18 Rules

Osaka City restricts standard 180-day Minpaku on weekdays within 100m of schools and inside strictly residential pockets. More importantly, Osaka City is Japan's primary National Strategic Special Zone (Tokku Minpaku / 特区民泊) hub allowing 365-day operation (minimum 2-night stay, >=25 sqm rooms) alongside full Simple Lodging permits—though new Tokku filings face heightened municipal scrutiny.

2. 365-Day Ryokan-gyo (簡易宿所) Parcel Feasibility

Chuo Ward (Shinsaibashi, Namba, Nipponbashi, Tanimachi) is overwhelmingly zoned Commercial, allowing both 1-night-minimum 365-day Simple Lodging (Kan'i Shukusho) and Special Zone 365-day apartments in buildings whose condo HOA bylaws explicitly authorize commercial hospitality.

Osaka City Public Health Center & Osaka Municipal Fire Department require building-wide Jikaho automatic fire alarms (and sprinklers if lodging floor space reaches statutory multi-tenant thresholds), emergency multilingual signage, and 10-minute emergency response.
