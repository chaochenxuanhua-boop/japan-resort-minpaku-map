Yes, foreign investors can legally purchase freehold property in Fukuoka City (Fukuoka) with zero nationality restrictions, but operating 365 days per year requires obtaining a Hotel and Ryokan Business Act Simple Lodging (Kan'i Shukusho / 簡易宿所) commercial permit from Fukuoka City Public Health & Medical Bureau - Living Hygiene Division (福岡市保健医療局生活衛生課). What are Fukuoka City Minpaku ordinance caps and restricted seasons?

Under Japan's Private Lodging Business Act (Article 18 local ordinance authority), Fukuoka City is classified as 'Amber: Zone/Season Restricted', resulting in ~165 effective operating days per year (versus the 180-day national ceiling). Specifically: Fukuoka City imposes strict neighborhood prior-notice, condo HOA written authorization proof, waste-disposal contracts, and school-zone rules on Minpaku filings rather than a citywide weekday blackout—yet high year-round Korean, Taiwanese, and domestic convention traffic makes a 180-day cap heavily restrictive.
