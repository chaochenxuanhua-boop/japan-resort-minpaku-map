Statutory Zoning, Fire Code & Official Ordinance Breakdown

1. Private Lodging Act (180d Minpaku) & Article 18 Rules

Taito Ward enforces one of Tokyo's toughest unhosted Minpaku ordinances: unless an administrator is stationed on-site (same building), operation is banned Monday noon through Saturday noon across the ward (~104 weekend nights/yr), protecting Asakusa and Ueno residential alleys.

2. 365-Day Ryokan-gyo (簡易宿所) Parcel Feasibility

Although nearly all of Asakusa and Kuramae sits in permissive Commercial zoning, Taito Ward's local Ryokan-gyo Ordinance mandates an on-site staffed front desk (Genkan Choba) with 24-hour personnel presence—blocking unmanned single-unit condos and favoring whole-building 4-to-12-suite boutique hotels.

3. Fire Service Act (Jikahō) & Building Standards

Taito Public Health Center rejects unmanned tablet-only check-in for most Simple Lodging conversions unless paired with continuous on-premises staff; Tokyo Fire Department requires interconnected Jikaho and smoke evacuation windows.

4. Accommodation Tax & Official Citations
