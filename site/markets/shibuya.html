Statutory Zoning, Fire Code & Official Ordinance Breakdown

Competent authority: Shibuya Ward Public Health Center Environmental Health Division (渋谷区保健所生活衛生課) · Verified 2026-11-26

1. Private Lodging Act (180d Minpaku) & Article 18 Rules

Shibuya Ward Ordinance severely restricts unhosted Minpaku in Exclusive Residential zones to school vacation windows only (roughly July 20-August 31 and late-December to early-January, ~65-70 days/yr), bans Bunkyo Educational Districts year-round, and requires prior neighbor notification.

2. 365-Day Ryokan-gyo (簡易宿所) Parcel Feasibility

365-day Ryokan-gyo (Simple Lodging or Boutique Hotel) is prohibited in Exclusive Residential pockets (Shoto, Yoyogi-Uehara residential blocks) and viable ONLY on Commercial, Neighborhood Commercial, or Class 2 Residential / Quasi-Industrial lots with full barrier-free and front-desk compliance.

3. Fire Service Act (Jikahō) & Building Standards

Shibuya Ward Public Health Center & Tokyo Fire Department enforce strict vertical fire compartmentation (Tokutei Shokaidan staircases on multi-story buildings), commercial Jikaho, 2-way egress, and staffed front-desk or ward-approved smart reception.

4. Accommodation Tax & Official Citations

Tokyo Metropolitan Accommodation Tax applies (¥100-¥200/person/night on nightly rates >= ¥10,000).
